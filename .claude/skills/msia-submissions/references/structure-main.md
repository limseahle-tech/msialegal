# Structure – Main Written Submissions

Derived from Caroline's filed samples (Plaintiff's WS opposing a transfer/consolidation application; Defendants' WS supporting an arbitration stay; Plaintiff's WS supporting an interim injunction).

## Front matter (both variants)

1. Court header, bold, centred, in Malay:
   - `DALAM MAHKAMAH TINGGI MALAYA DI KUALA LUMPUR` (or `DALAM MAHKAMAH TINGGI DI KUALA LUMPUR`)
   - `DALAM WILAYAH PERSEKUTUAN KUALA LUMPUR, MALAYSIA`
   - Division, e.g. `(BAHAGIAN DAGANG)` when applicable
   - `GUAMAN SIVIL NO.: WA-22IP-21-02/2026` — bold, underlined
2. `ANTARA` → plaintiff(s) table (name, company/IC no. in brackets, role `...PLAINTIF` / `...PLAINTIF-PLAINTIF`) → `DAN` → defendants table (numbered 1., 2., … role `...DEFENDAN` / `...DEFENDAN-DEFENDAN`).
3. Boxed title: `PLAINTIFF'S WRITTEN SUBMISSIONS` (bold) with italic subtitle `(in support of Enclosure 14)` / `(in opposing Enclosure 16)`. Short subtitle only.
4. Solicitors block: our firm (name, address, `[Ref.: …]`) and, for an opposing party, theirs side by side.
5. Title repeated in the body, bold underlined, italic subtitle beneath, then: `May it please this Honourable Court,`
6. Long submissions (roughly 25+ pages, e.g. injunctions): insert a Table of Contents after the front matter and use lettered sections (A., B., C1., C2., E1.…).

## Body

Top-level headings: **<u>BOLD UNDERLINED CAPS</u>**, optionally lettered (`A. INTRODUCTION`). Sub-headings in the argument: **BOLD CAPS, NOT UNDERLINED**, phrased as a proposition. Third level (inside a sub-heading): ***bold italic, sentence case***.

### INTRODUCTION

1. "This is the [Party]'s Written Submissions filed in [support of / opposing] the [Party]'s Notice of Application dated [d.m.yyyy] in Enclosure [X], *inter alia,* pursuant to ***<u>[rules]</u>*** and/or the inherent jurisdiction of this Honourable Court, for the following orders:-" → prayers as (a), (b), each closed with its defined term in bold within quotes, e.g. (collectively referred to as "**Stay Order**").
2. "For ease of reference:-" → (a) the 1st Defendant is hereinafter referred to as "**D1**"; … (f) D1 to D5 collectively referred to as "**the Defendants**"; (g) defined terms adopted from the SOC/affidavits (with the Malay equivalent in italics where the pleadings are in Malay); (h) the BOA definition:
   > the Plaintiff's Bundle of Authorities filed together herewith is hereinafter referred to as "**PBOA**" **[Enclosure __]**. References will be made based on the respective Enclosure numbers and the corresponding page numbers of each Enclosure. For instance, "E1/2" is page 2 of Enclosure 1 (PDF pagination number).
3. "We refer to the following cause papers: -" with underlined bold group labels (**<u>Pleadings</u>**, **<u>Enclosure 16</u>**), each item: document, date, **[Enclosure X]**, ("**short form**").

### Variant A – Applicant (in support)

- **B. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY** — what the application achieves in one paragraph; the agreements/rights it rests on (with E-cites); the key undisputed facts and admissions ("It is not disputed that …"); why the statutory exceptions are not made out; a closing "In the premises, … ought to …" paragraph.
- **C. SALIENT FACTS** — chronological, each fact with E-cite; quote operative clauses "(*in verbatim*)" with the decisive words **<u>bold underlined</u>**. For long fact sections use C1, C2 … sub-headings by subject.
- **D. THE APPLICABLE LAW** — set out the test as (a)-(d) limbs; **<u>Authorities</u>**: (i), (ii), (iii) in court hierarchy, each with a short quoted paragraph; burden of proof and its shift.
- **E. THE [PARTY]'S SUBMISSION** — open with "In essence, we humbly submit that:" (a), (b); then one proposition-heading per limb; anticipate the other side's affidavit points under each limb, not in a separate section.
- **F. CONCLUSION** — "Wherefore, we humbly pray for order in terms of the said Application via Enclosure [X]" (plus costs where sought).

### Variant B – Respondent (in opposing)

- **CRUX** — para 1: the other side's basis in neutral terms ("The said Application … essentially is made on the basis that …"). Para 2: "We respectfully submit that the said Application by D1 is wholly misconceived and without any basis in law or fact and ought to be dismissed *in limine*, amongst others, based on the following grounds:" → lettered grounds, the decisive one in bold. Final para: "Based on the aforesaid reasons, we humbly submit that **[core proposition]**. Therefore, the said Application ought to be dismissed. We further submit in details as follows."
- **RELEVANT LAWS** — "At the outset, it is convenient and prudent to set out the relevant law governing …": quote each rule (italic, key words bold/underlined, "(emphasis added)"); "It is trite that …" principle; **<u>Authorities</u>**; then reduce the law to numbered conditions ("Firstly, … Secondly, …") and finish "none of the aforesaid conditions are satisfied by D1 on the facts of this case".
- **[PARTY]'S MAIN SUBMISSIONS** — if one prayer depends on another (e.g. a stay pending transfer), say so first and argue the substantive prayer only. Then one proposition-heading per limb. Inside each: topic sentence ("It is submitted that …"), the other side's allegation quoted in italics with pinpoint, "are misconceived based on the following reasons:" (a)… , authority, and close "Based on the aforesaid reasons, it is therefore submitted that …". Use a comparison table where it shows the difference faster than prose (Aspect | This Action | Other Suit).
- **Abuse / tactical-motive section** (only when supported by the record) — last substantive section.
- **CONCLUSION** — "Based on the aforesaid reasons, it is submitted that D1 has failed to demonstrate any valid grounds for … to be granted by this Honourable Court." / "Wherefore, we respectfully pray that the said Application be dismissed with costs to be paid by D1 to the Plaintiff."

## Back matter

- `Dated this 26th of May, 2026.`
- Signature block on the right: `(signed)`, dotted line, `Solicitors for the Plaintiff`, `Messrs Lum Kok Kiong & Co`.
- Filed-by paragraph: "This **PLAINTIFF'S WRITTEN SUBMISSIONS** *(in opposing Enclosure 16)* is filed by **Messrs Lum Kok Kiong & Co**, solicitors for the Plaintiff having its address for service at …" followed by Tel/Fax/Email/Ref in square brackets.
