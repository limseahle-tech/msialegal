# Style, Phrasing and Citation Conventions

## Voice

- First person plural, addressed to the Court: "We respectfully submit…", "We humbly submit…", "It is submitted that…", "We wish to emphasise / highlight…", "We find support from the case of…", "We refer to the Court of Appeal decision in…".
- Assertive conclusions. The other side's position is "misconceived", "fundamentally misconceived", "wholly misconceived and without any basis in law or fact", "an afterthought", "cannot stand", "does not assist D1's case". Do not hedge unless the law genuinely requires it.
- "It is trite that…" / "It is settled law that…" introduce an uncontroversial principle, always followed by the authority.
- "Based on the aforesaid reasons, …" closes a section. "In the premises, …" closes a summary. "In any event, …" introduces an alternative. "Critically, …" / "Crucially, …" sparingly, at most once per section.
- Defined-term references: "the said Application", "the said Injunction Application", "the action herein", "the Suit herein", "this Honourable Court". Write "D1" without "the" ("D1's Submissions", not "the D1's Submissions").
- British/Malaysian spelling, one form throughout: emphasise, manoeuvre, characterise, judgment.
- Dates d.m.yyyy (13.4.2026); ordinals with superscript (1st Defendant); "Dated this 26th of May, 2026."

## Emphasis hierarchy (in order of strength)

1. **Bold** — the proposition the Judge must take away from the paragraph (usually one clause per paragraph).
2. **<u>Bold underlined</u>** — the operative words inside a quoted provision, clause or judgment.
3. **<u>CAPS bold underlined</u>** (**<u>NO</u>**, **<u>NEVER</u>**) — at most once or twice per document.
4. *Italic* — the other side's words quoted back, Latin (*inter alia*, *in limine*, *bona fide*, *status quo*), and all quotations of authority.

## Citation formats

| Item | Format |
|---|---|
| Case, first mention | ***<u>Central Securities (Holdings) Bhd v Haron Mohamed Zaid [1979] 2 MLJ 244 at 251 (FC)</u>*** **[Tab 3 of PBOA, E24/17]** |
| Case, later mention | ***<u>Central Securities (supra)</u>*** **[Tab 3 of PBOA, E24/17]** |
| Paragraph pin-cite | *(see paragraphs 18 & 26)* — italic only, after the citation |
| Statute / rule | ***<u>Order 57 Rule 1 of the ROC 2012</u>*** **[Tab 1 of PBOA, E24/5]**; ***<u>Section 10(1) of the Arbitration Act 2005</u>*** **[Tab 1 of DBOA, E13/4]** |
| Court suffix | (FC), (CA), (HC), (HOL), (COA where the report uses it) |
| Affidavit / exhibit | [*kindly refer to **Exhibit "CFC-1"** of DAIS at **E8/10-34***] |
| Paragraph of affidavit | [*see paragraphs 7 to 20 and 34 of DAIS at **E17/4-9&12***] |
| Other side's submissions | [*refer to paragraphs 26 to 28 and 35 of D1's Submissions, **E26/12,13&15***] |
| Own earlier submissions | [refer to paragraphs 13 to 26 of the Plaintiff's Submissions at E59/5-7] |
| Inline authority inside a bracket | [*refer to ***<u>Section 20 of the Companies Act 2016</u>*** at **Tab 8 of PBOA, E24/67***] |

E-page ranges: `E24/30&31` for two separate pages, `E24/122-124` for a run, `E26/12,13&15` for a list.

## Authorities block

Lead into it with an underlined label on its own line: **<u>Authority</u>:** (one) or **<u>Authorities</u>:-** (several), then:

```
(i)  [[Case [year] vol REPORT page (Court)]] [Tab n of PBOA, En/p], the Federal Court held at paragraph x as follows:-

     "[x] … **key words** … **__decisive phrase__** …" (emphasis added)
```

- Quote the shortest passage that carries the point. Use "…" for omissions, keep the paragraph number in square brackets.
- After the quote, one sentence applying it to our facts, unless the next paragraph does so.
- Order: Federal Court → Court of Appeal → High Court → foreign.

## Paragraph construction

- One proposition per numbered paragraph. Lettered (a), (b) for limbs, grounds or evidence; roman (i), (ii) beneath that. Introduce a list with ":-".
- Quote the other side's allegation in italics with its pinpoint, then answer it. Do not paraphrase it into a weaker version they can disown.
- Group several of their allegations answered by one reason into one paragraph with sub-points.
- Use a table for side-by-side comparison (parties, causes of action, subject matter, reliefs, forum).
- End every argument section with a one-sentence conclusion tied to the limb of the test.

## Placeholders and drafting notes

- Unknown Enclosure, Tab or page: `[Enclosure ==XX==]`, `[Tab ==_== of PBOA, E==_/_==]`. Highlighted yellow, never left plain.
- A fact still to be verified: highlight the whole sentence and add a Word comment saying what must be checked and where (e.g. "check minutes of CM on 11.9.2026"). Caroline gives her team instructions this way.
