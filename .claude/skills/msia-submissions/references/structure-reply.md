# Structure – Submissions in Reply

Derived from Caroline's filed replies (Plaintiff's Reply opposing a transfer/consolidation application; Defendants' Reply supporting an arbitration stay). A reply answers the other side's submissions. It does not re-run our main submissions.

## Front matter

Same as the main submission, title `PLAINTIFF'S SUBMISSIONS IN REPLY` / `DEFENDANTS' SUBMISSIONS IN REPLY`, subtitle `(in opposing Enclosure 16)` / `(in support of Enclosure 7)`.

## Body

### INTRODUCTION (3 paragraphs)

1. "These are the [Party]'s Submissions in Reply to the [Other Party]'s Written Submission dated [date] **[Enclosure X]** and in further [opposing / support of] the [Party]'s Notice of Application dated [date] **[Enclosure Y]**."
2. "We refer to: -" (i) our Written Submissions dated … **[Enclosure]** ("**Plaintiff's Submissions**") together with our BOA **[Enclosure]** ("**PBOA**"); (ii) their Written Submission dated … **[Enclosure]** ("**D1's Submissions**") with their BOA ("**DBOA**"). Note any defect in their bundle briefly (e.g. "*however we notice that the relevant judgment of the respective case authorities enclosed in the DBOA was not highlighted*").
3. "For ease of reference:-" (a) "All the abbreviations and/or references used in the **[our own]** Submissions are adopted herein, unless otherwise stated; and" (b) the BOA (2) definition with the E1/2 explanation.

> Check 3(a) names **our own** submissions. It is easy to carry over the other party's name from a template.

### PRELIMINARY OBJECTION (only where it applies)

Use where their submissions raise issues not deposed to in any affidavit.

- "We wish to raise a preliminary objection to the following issues which were never raised before in the [affidavits] and therefore constitute a **statement from the bar**, especially when the [Party] has not been afforded any opportunity to address the said Issues in its affidavit." → (a), (b) each with their paragraph and E-cite → define "(collectively known as "**the Unpleaded Issues**")".
- **<u>Authorities:-</u>** standard set (verify Tab/E for each new bundle):
  - ***<u>Syed Omar bin Syed Mohamed v Perbadanan Nasional Bhd [2013] 1 MLJ 461 (FC)</u>*** — para [19]
  - ***<u>Ng Hee Thoong & Anor v Public Bank Bhd [1995] 1 MLJ 281 at 287 (CA)</u>***
  - ***<u>Malayan Banking Bhd (formerly known as 'Mayban Finance Bhd') v Boo Hock Soon @ Boo Choo Soon [2013] 2 MLJ 843 at 854 (CA)</u>*** — para [35]
- "Without prejudice to the aforesaid preliminary objection, we further submit as follows."
- When the same point is answered later on the merits, say only that it is answered without prejudice to the objection. Do not repeat the objection.

### CRUX (2-3 paragraphs maximum)

Pick the pattern that fits:

- **Respondent:** "In essence, the central contention underpinning D1's Submissions is that … — to which we respectfully submit that this allegation is fundamentally misconceived." + the one-sentence reason (in bold) + "We reiterate the Plaintiff's Submissions in their entirety and maintain that **[core proposition]**."
- **Applicant:** "We wish to emphasise at the outset that the Plaintiffs do not and cannot dispute the following material facts: -" (a)-(d), then "We wish to highlight that the Plaintiffs did not reply specifically to the Defendants' Submissions and have failed to raise any valid or legally sustainable grounds to resist …, for the following reasons:-" with one bold label per ground (**Non-arbitrability / public policy issue**: …).

### [PARTY]'S SUBMISSIONS IN REPLY

- Sub-headings follow **the other side's headings and order**, re-labelled from our side: "THE ALLEGED LEGAL FRAMEWORK", "THE ALLEGED SUBJECT MATTER OF THE DISPUTE", "NO OVERLAP OF WITNESSES AND DOCUMENTS".
- Where they cite the governing law first, open with a legal-position section: "While the Plaintiff does not dispute the principles under ***<u>[rule]</u>*** or ***<u>[case]</u>*** cited in paragraphs 11 to 13 of D1's Submissions [*refer to **E26/5-6***], it is submitted that they do not assist D1's case …"
- **Paragraph pattern:** "D1 in paragraphs [x] of D1's Submissions [*refer to **E26/…***] alleged that "*[their words]*". We reiterate paragraphs [y] of the Plaintiff's Submissions [*refer to **E25/…***] and submit that such allegations are misconceived and do not assist D1's case [for the following reasons:-]".
- **Distinguishing their authorities:** list them in one paragraph with Tab/E-cites, then "(a) In ***<u>[Case] (supra)</u>***, the Court … specifically because …" one sub-point per case, then "In stark contrast, …". Where their own authority is against them, say so: "It is significant to note that this authority … would militate against D1 …", or cite their own headnote/paragraph back to them.
- **Afterthought points:** "… is an afterthought and does not assist D1's case. We wish to emphasise that this issue was never raised by D1 before in the DAIS or DAIR."
- For any point already fully argued in our main submissions: cross-refer ("We reiterate paragraphs 15 to 36 of the Plaintiff's Submissions [refer to E25/14-25]") and add only the new answer.

### CONCLUSION

- "Based on the aforesaid reasons and the Plaintiff's Submissions, it is submitted that D1 has failed to demonstrate any valid grounds that justify the granting of … by this Honourable Court."
- Respondent: "Wherefore, we respectfully pray that the said Application be dismissed with costs to be paid by D1 to the Plaintiff."
- Applicant: "On the contrary, the Defendants had sufficiently proven that … Accordingly, this Honourable Court ought to … Wherefore, we humbly pray for order in terms of the said Application via Enclosure 7."

## Back matter

Same as the main submission, with the title `[PARTY]'S SUBMISSIONS IN REPLY` in the filed-by paragraph.
