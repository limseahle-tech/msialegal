# Application Playbooks

Skeletons for the applications that appear in Caroline's filed submissions. Each lists the legal test to set out, the order of argument that worked, and the authorities she has used.

**Every authority below is a starting point, not a verified citation.** Before using one, confirm on Lexis (via `lexis-my-research`) that it is good law, get the report it is in, and find the exact page in the new bundle. Never copy a Tab/E-page from an old matter.

## 1. Summary judgment – Order 14 ROC 2012 (Plaintiff)

**Order of sections:** INTRODUCTION → BRIEF BACKGROUND FACTS (bold-italic sub-heads per cause of action, plus *Notice to the Defendant*) → LAW ON SUMMARY JUDGMENT → CRUX → one section per cause of action (bold underlined caps) → NO TRIABLE ISSUES & NO MERITORIOUS DEFENCE → CONCLUSION.

- **Law first, then CRUX.** Set out the three preliminary requirements (appearance entered, SOC served, AIS complies with O.14 r.2), then "the burden shifts to the Defendant".
- **CRUX:** "the material facts forming the basis of the Plaintiffs' claims … are **<u>not disputed</u>** by the Defendant whereby amongst others:-" with the admissions from the Defence and affidavits (paragraph and E-cite each).
- **Each cause of action:** statutory provision → elements (quote the case listing them) → apply element by element in an (i)-(iv) list → "Accordingly, all the elements … are satisfied".
- **No triable issues:** quote the rule that bare denials and equivocal assertions are rejected → list *their* issues (a)-(f) with AIR paragraph and E-cite → "With respect, the aforesaid are **not genuine triable issues but mere assertions which <u>do not displace the material and undisputed facts</u>**" → answer each briefly.
- **Supporting material:** Annexures to the submissions (list of marks, screenshots with the infringing part circled in red) and short tables for sales and advertising figures, each with its exhibit E-cite under the table.
- **Prayer:** "In the premises, the Plaintiffs humbly pray for an order in terms of the Plaintiffs' Summary Judgment Application."

Authorities used: *National Company for Foreign Trade v Kayu Raya* [1984] 2 MLJ 300 (FC); *Cempaka Finance v Ho Lai Ying* [2006] 3 CLJ 544 (FC); *Bank Negara Malaysia v Mohd Ismail* [1992] 1 MLRA 190 (SC); *Voo Min En v Leong Chung Fatt* (FC); *Puncak Niaga v NZ Wheels* [2012] 1 MLJ 27 (CA); *Fabrique Ebel v Syarikat Perniagaan Tukang Jam City Port* [1988] 1 MLJ 188 (injunction by summary judgment). For trade marks and passing off: ss 9, 52, 54, 159 TMA 2019; *Leo Pharmaceutical v Kotra Pharma* [2009] 5 MLJ 703; *Ortus Expert White v Nor Yanni* [2022] 2 MLJ 67 (FC); *Reckitt & Colman v Borden* [1990] 1 All ER 873 (HL); *Seet Chuan Seng v Tee Yih Jia* [1994] 2 MLJ 770 (SC); *Yong Sze Fun v Syarikat Zamani Hj Tamin* [2012] 1 MLJ 585 (CA); *Parker-Knoll v Knoll International* [1962] RPC 265 (HL); *Acushnet v Metro Gold* [2006] 7 CLJ 557 (strict liability).

## 3. Stay pending arbitration – s 10 Arbitration Act 2005 (Applicant)

Covered in `structure-main.md` Variant A. Core moves: the stay is mandatory once the s 10(1) preconditions are met; there is no residual discretion (the "sufficient reason" discretion under s 6 AA 1952 was abolished); the Court looks at the substance of the pleaded claim, not its form; non-signatories do not defeat the stay; fraud and conspiracy are arbitrable; multiplicity of proceedings is no answer.

Authorities used: *Press Metal Sarawak v Etiqa Takaful* [2016] 5 MLJ 417 (FC); *Setia Awan Management v SPNB Aspirasi* [2025] 4 MLJ 181 (CA); *ZAQ Construction v Putrasudhie Holdings* [2014] 10 MLJ 633.

## 5. Setting aside a Bankruptcy Notice / Creditor's Petition – Insolvency Act 1967 (Judgment Debtor)

**Order of sections:** INTRODUCTION (statutory basis and prayers listed) → RELEVANT BACKGROUND FACTS (one dated event per paragraph: "On 7.10.2025, JC filed …") → RELEVANT LAWS → JD'S MAIN SUBMISSIONS → THE DEFECTS CANNOT BE CURED (s 131) → STAY OF PROCEEDINGS PENDING DISPOSAL → CONCLUSION.

- **RELEVANT LAWS:** "We submit that [four] statutory planks frame the JD's Application herein:-" then 10.1 *Firstly* … 10.2 *Secondly* … (decimal sub-numbering: builder `sublevel: decimal`). Close with the strict-construction principle: bankruptcy is quasi-penal and the statute is construed in favour of the debtor (*Sobri bin Arshad v Associated Tractors* [1991] 3 MLJ 32).
- **Main submissions:** a bold-caps heading per instrument (DEFECTIVE, FLAWED AND/OR IRREGULAR BN / CP). Open each with "In essence, the JD's case here is that … based on the following grounds:-" (13.1 *First* … 13.4 *Fourth*, each with AIS/AIR pinpoints). Then a bold-italic proposition heading per ground ("*Service of the said BN was not perfected*").
- **Typical grounds:** underlying judgment and Writ not served; BN not personally served and substituted service not perfected; correction affidavit filed after the event cannot cure; wrong state (Practice Direction No. 3 of 1993); act of bankruptcy date wrong; CP Affidavit defective (O.41 r.3 / Rule 38 jurat for an illiterate deponent; CP not annexed; no authority under seal, s 133(a) / Rule 215).

## 6. Stay of bankruptcy proceedings pending appeal – s 97 Insolvency Act 1967 (Judgment Debtor)

Order of argument (used in Malay):
1. **Test:** "sufficient reason", not "special circumstances" (*Re Rosli Abdul Kadir* [2008] 5 CLJ 691; *Chen Ying v Export-Import Bank* [2016] 7 CLJ 475 (CA); *Re Thangarajoo Vyran* [2013] 1 CLJ 286; *Liew Hon Kong v Bank Pertanian* [2020] MLJU 701). Alternatively, even if special circumstances apply, an appeal rendered nugatory suffices (*Kosma Palm Oil Mill v Koperasi Serbausaha Makmur* (FC)).
2. The appeal has merit. List the appeal issues as "Sama ada …" (whether) questions.
3. The appeal will be rendered nugatory, futile and academic.
4. Irreversible prejudice to the JD: the statutory consequences of bankruptcy (*Poh Gaik Lye v Amfraser Securities* [2015] 1 MLJ 453 (CA); *Lim Boon Kiak v Affin Bank* [2013] 5 MLRA 381 (CA)).
5. The stay is temporary and the JC suffers no prejudice: the judgment and interest stand; the appeal hearing date is near (*Ber Chang Kian Chiang* [2024] MLJU 925).
6. Ad interim stay. State what happened at case management **only after checking the court minutes** (see the checklist).
